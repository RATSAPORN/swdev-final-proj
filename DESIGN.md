# Browser interface

Mode: Operate. The primary task is choosing a massage shop and reserving a date.

Use an uncluttered warm paper surface, dark forest-green actions, charcoal text, and muted terracotta accents. The app has a compact top navigation, a two-column shop-and-booking workspace, and a full-width reservation list. On mobile, the booking panel follows the shop list and forms stack. Shop entries are separated by rules rather than nested cards. Empty, loading, validation and expired-session states use plain actionable text.

Tokens: paper #f6f4ed, surface #ffffff, ink #23352c, muted #5c6860, accent #285442, border #d8ded5, danger #a23328. Body type: system sans-serif for operational clarity; title type: Georgia serif. Controls have 6px corners, 44px minimum height, and visible keyboard focus. Space scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px. No decorative imagery is needed for this task-oriented surface.
